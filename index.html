HOW TO RACE

Every driver has the same shuffled 1–5 movement deck. Flip simultaneously, then all cars move. ⚑ Flags draw a surprise event; ⚡ boosts grant an extra flip. Pick routes at forks. A shared space causes a crash. Pit on the next approach to the lap line when required.

FLIP CARS

Every flip is a race. Every route is a gamble.

Up to four local drivers race toy cars around a winding circuit. Take alternate routes, pull risky flag cards, stockpile defensive items, dodge collisions, and complete 3 laps first.

Local keyboard controls: Red Q / W · Blue A / S · Yellow K / L · Green N / M. First key = flip / select. Second = cycle route. Single player can use Space / →. On tablets, use the two on-screen buttons at each player's station.

Identical movement decks: 1 × 8, 2 × 7, 3 × 5, 4 × 4, 5 × 3 (27 cards per driver). Decks reshuffle automatically.
